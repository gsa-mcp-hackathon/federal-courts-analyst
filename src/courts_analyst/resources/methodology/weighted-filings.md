# Weighted filings per judgeship

Weighted filings multiply each case by a Federal Judicial Center case weight
(the relative judge time its type needs) and divide the total by authorized
judgeships. The Judicial Conference uses them when it assesses the need for
judgeships: it generally looks for more than 430 per judgeship before
recommending one, and 600 marks a judicial emergency where there is a vacancy.

- Judicial Business Table X-1A publishes them by district, first, for each
  fiscal year. Judicial Facts and Figures Table 6.2 publishes the national
  series over many years.
- Starting with the 2023 edition, Table 6.2 revised the weighted civil series
  down for recent years (FY2020: 554 → 322). The tables' notes don't explain
  why. This server reports both figures with their editions and labels any
  explanation of the revision as inference.
- Territorial courts are outside the case-weight studies, so no weighted
  figures are published for them.
- The current case weights (FJC 2016 study) are not fully published, so
  weighted figures come only from the official tables, never from the IDB.
