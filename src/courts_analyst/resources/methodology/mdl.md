# Multidistrict litigation and direct filing

The Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation consolidates civil cases with
common facts before one transferee court. Cases reach it two ways:

- **Transferred** by the Panel (IDB origin 6). Table X-1A excludes these from
  filings per judgeship; Table C-1's pending counts include them.
- **Filed directly** in the MDL court (IDB origin 13, coded from July 1,
  2016). Before that such cases were origin 1 and can't be separated.

Direct filing can put tens of thousands of cases in one district in one year.
In FY2020, 202,463 cases were filed directly in N.D. Fla., almost all in MDL
2885 (3M Combat Arms earplugs), which drove the national jump in civil
filings. `explain_change` reports each change with and without directly filed
MDL cases.
