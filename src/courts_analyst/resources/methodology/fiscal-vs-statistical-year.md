# Fiscal years and statistical years

- **Fiscal year**: October 1 to September 30, named for the year it ends
  (FY2020 = Oct 1, 2019 – Sep 30, 2020). Every recent table in the catalog
  reports fiscal years.
- **Statistical year**: the older 12-month period ending June 30. Judicial
  Facts and Figures' 1990 rows are statistical year 1990 (Jul 1989 – Jun
  1990) and are flagged `period_type: statistical_year`.
- The IDB's AO dates (`FDATEUSE`, `TDATEUSE`) place a case in the fiscal year
  the AO counted it; durations use the actual filing and termination dates.
- The catalog holds September 30 periods only for recent years; June 30,
  December 31 and March 31 tables are not loaded.
